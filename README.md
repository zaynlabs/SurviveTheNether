# 🔥 Survive the Nether

**Survive the Nether** flips Minecraft survival upside down: you wake up in a **Crimson Forest** and your only mission is to lock in, finesse the Piglins, survive the chaotic trenches, build a portal, and **touch grass in the Overworld**!

---

## ✨ What Does This Mod Do?

* **🌋 Crimson Forest Spawn:** You start directly inside a Crimson Forest on solid ground. The spawn finder makes sure you never spawn swimming in lava.
* **🔁 Die in the Nether, Stay in the Nether:** You fell in fire with no anchor? Bro really thought he was getting a free ticket to the Overworld 😭. You respawn right back in the Nether.
* **🛏️ Anchors & Beds actually work:** Charge up a Respawn Anchor to lock in your base coordinates. If you manage to escape to the Overworld and sleep in a bed, you respawn at your bed like a certified boss.
* **🪵 Pure Vanilla Gameplay:** Zero custom items or complex commands. Everything is 100% vanilla Minecraft survival using what's already in the game.

---

## 🧭 How to Not Get Cooked

### 1. Punch Trees or It's Over
* **Punch Crimson Stems:** Crimson and Warped trees give you wood. Craft workbenches, sticks, and your first wooden pickaxe.
* **Mine Blackstone:** Blackstone is literally Cobblestone's little brother. Mine it to get stone tools, swords, and furnaces right away.
* **Mushroom Soup meta:** Pick red and brown mushrooms from the floor and craft wooden bowls. Infinite safe food so Hoglins don't eat you on day one.

### 2. Drip Check & Piglin Diplomacy
* **Craft Gold Boots IMMEDIATELY:** Mine Nether gold ore, smelt it, and put on gold boots unless you want the entire Piglin squad jumping you.

### 3. Bartering & Getting Stacked
* **Gamble with Piglins:** Throw gold ingots at Piglins to get the essentials:
  * **Gravel:** Dig it for flint.
  * **Iron Nuggets:** Craft iron ingots for shields, buckets, and flint & steel.
  * **Fire Resistance Potions:** Cheat codes against sudden lava falls.
  * **Obsidian:** The only way to escape this battlefield.
* **Raid Bastions:** Pull up on Bastion Remnants to find chests packed with gold blocks, iron gear and obsidian.
* **Respawn Anchors:** Craft an anchor using 6 Crying Obsidian and 3 Glowstone blocks to set your main base checkpoint.

### 4. The Final Escape (We Are So Back)
* Collect **10 Obsidian blocks** from Piglin barters or Bastion chests.
* Craft a **Flint and Steel** using bartered iron and flint.
* Build your portal, light it up, and step through to reach the Overworld for the very first time!

## 📥 Installation

1. Download and install **[Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/)**.
2. Drop the **Survive the Nether** `.jar` into your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
3. Make sure **Fabric API** is also in your mods folder.
4. Launch the game, create a world, and survive the grind!
